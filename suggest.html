Submissions

Suggest a piece for the next volume.

Volume 02 publishes in Q3 2026. Submissions, recommendations, and respectful disagreement are welcome from practitioners, editors, and readers who have found something worth including in the next index.

Pieces are evaluated on the strength of the argument and the specificity of the perspective, not the size of the brand or the popularity of the author. Editorial notes accompany every entry, including notes that disagree with what the piece argues.

Particularly seeking

  • Practitioner essays from senior technical marketers, technical product marketers, and solutions marketing leaders inside the major enterprise software categories: hyperscaler and cloud infrastructure, data and analytics platforms, cybersecurity, DevOps and developer tools, AI and ML platforms, workflow and platform software, and observability
  • Sharp framework or methodology pieces that go beyond surface-level definitions of the discipline
  • Named-author analysis of where the field is shifting under AI pressure
  • Historical perspective on how the technical marketing function has evolved across the major enterprise software eras
  • Counter-arguments to currently popular framings of the discipline

Especially interested in submissions from practitioners at AWS, Snowflake, ServiceNow, Salesforce, Palo Alto Networks, GitHub, Anthropic, Cloudflare, Datadog, Confluent, MongoDB, HashiCorp, Atlassian, and their peers across these categories.

What gets declined

Vendor content marketing pieces, generic listicles, AI-generated explainers, and pieces that primarily exist to drive traffic to a commercial offer. The index is curated, not comprehensive. Every entry needs to earn its place by saying something true that isn't being said elsewhere.
