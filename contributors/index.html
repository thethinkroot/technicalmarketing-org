Contributors

The people behind the work.

Original pieces. Sourced arguments. Names on record.

Every piece published in Technical Marketing carries a name. Practitioner authority means accountability: for the argument made, the evidence cited, the position held. The contributors listed here have put their names on work that advances the discipline's working definition.

Inclusion is based on editorial judgment. The strength of the argument and the specificity of the perspective are the only criteria. Brand size, company name, and title are not. A principal engineer at a fifty-person startup and a director at a public platform company are evaluated on the same terms. The editorial decisions that put each name on this page are documented at /about.

Volume III · Q1 2027

The next volume opens the contributor pipeline to the field. Senior practitioners working in technical marketing or adjacent disciplines are invited to submit original pieces for consideration. The standard is the same as for everything else this publication produces: a clear argument, specific evidence, a perspective that advances the discipline's working definition rather than describing it from the outside.

The conversations the field still needs to have are marked by the empty spaces in the quarterly index. Those spaces are the editorial brief for Volume III.
