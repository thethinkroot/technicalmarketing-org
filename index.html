Editor's note

Technical marketing is practiced under many names across enterprise software. At AWS it sits inside product marketing. At ServiceNow it operates as technical product marketing. At smaller vendors it blends with developer relations. The variations are real, but they describe one underlying discipline.

This index gathers the field's current thinking — definitions, frameworks, career perspectives, and the tensions that will define what the work becomes. New volumes publish quarterly. Suggestions and submissions are welcome.
