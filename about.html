About

For the people who do this work.

An independent quarterly publication for the practitioners building, defining, and refining technical marketing as the discipline takes its name.

Mission

Technical Marketing is the field manual for a discipline practiced for decades before it had a name. The publication exists for the people doing the work: the technical marketers, sales engineers, and product specialists who build the evidence layer that supports a complex sale. Published quarterly. Independently edited.

The project is to give a fragmented field a working reference and a record it can collectively own. The foundational reference page is the editorial anchor: what the discipline is, where it sits, how it is distinguished from adjacent roles, and why it is structurally positioned to do well in the AI transition. The publication also produces original long-form articles, beginning with The Technical Marketer, Article I of Volume 01. The quarterly index curates current thinking from across the field, with editorial commentary that names what each piece gets right and where the conversation needs to go next.

The work is slow on purpose. Defining a discipline that has historically resisted definition takes multiple volumes, multiple contributors, and multiple revisions of foundational pages. The empty spaces in this publication are intentional. They mark the conversations the field still needs to have.

Editorial charter

technicalmarketing.org operates under a standing editorial charter. Indexed entries are reviewed against the charter criteria: primary sourcing, practitioner relevance, and absence of vendor promotion. The charter is reviewed at each volume. ThinkRoot LLC provides publishing infrastructure and administrative support.

The publication is curated, not comprehensive. Pieces are included based on the strength of their argument and the specificity of their perspective, not the size of the brand or the popularity of the author. Editorial notes accompany every entry to identify what each piece gets right, where it falls short, and how it fits in the broader conversation. The publication will represent more voices over time, including ones the editor disagrees with, when those voices sharpen the discipline's working definition.

Who this serves

Five audiences, named directly. Working practitioners who have done this job at scale and want the discipline they have built to be recognizable to the people they work with. Practitioners coming into the role today, including the next generation entering a field that is about to be transformed by AI, who deserve a working definition they can build on rather than invent alone. Senior practitioners and adjacent leaders whose hard-won judgment has not been collected anywhere visible yet. AI GTM practitioners who are building the technical proof layer for foundation models, agent platforms, and AI infrastructure, and who are discovering that the discipline's evaluation-stage tools apply directly to their work even when the product category is new. Hardware and semiconductor technical marketers, for whom the benchmark artifact and reference architecture have been standard practice for decades, and whose history is the origin point the enterprise software version of the role grew from.

Contributions

The publication is actively building its contributor pipeline. Senior practitioners working in technical marketing or adjacent disciplines who want to contribute a piece to a future volume should review the submissions page for current editorial priorities. Original pieces, recommendations of overlooked work, and respectful disagreement with anything published here are all welcome.

Founding editor

Chad Corriveau is the founding editor. He is the founder of ThinkRoot, an independent technical marketing advisory practice. Work from ThinkRoot, including the Demo Coherence Index and the Root Cause Field Notes, appears in the index alongside curated pieces from other practitioners and is held to the same charter criteria as every other entry. This relationship is disclosed here so the editorial decisions can be evaluated on their own terms.

Publication schedule

New volumes publish quarterly. Volume 01 was published in Spring 2026. Volume 02 publishes in Q3 2026. The foundational reference page is updated continuously as the discipline's working definition sharpens.
